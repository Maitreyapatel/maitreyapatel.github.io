I am a Research Scientist at [Adobe](https://www.adobe.com/), working on generative models for visual content creation. Previously, I completed my Ph.D. at Arizona State University, advised by [Yezhou Yang](https://yezhouyang.engineering.asu.edu) and [Chitta Baral](https://cogintlab-asu.github.io/).

My research is focused on building <span style="color: #4CAF50;">unified generative models</span> (omni models) — single architectures that natively understand and generate across modalities — and on the <span style="color: #2196F3;">large-scale training infrastructure</span> that makes them possible. This spans <span style="color: #4CAF50;">architecture design</span> for unified tokenization and multimodal generation, <span style="color: #2196F3;">pre-training</span> and <span style="color: #2196F3;">RL alignment</span> at scale, and `inference-time steering` for controllability and reliability. **I believe true World Models must be generalizable, efficient, controllable, responsible, and grounded in physical laws.**
